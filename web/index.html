Featured R&D Project

Applied Research · Underground Communication

LinkMine

Intelligent Communication & Safety Infrastructure for Underground Mining

LinkMine is an applied research project exploring resilient underground communication, intelligent safety monitoring, and distributed sensing for underground mining environments. Depth, tunnels, physical obstructions, and shifting infrastructure make communication in these environments genuinely difficult — LinkMine investigates how distributed nodes, sensors, edge computing, and intelligent software could work together to improve situational awareness and communication resilience.

Resilient Wireless Networks

Research into communication architectures that can maintain connectivity across complex underground environments.

Mesh Networking

Distributed nodes that relay information through the underground environment.

Intelligent Safety Monitoring

Sensors and data analysis to help identify unusual conditions and potentially dangerous events.

Edge Computing

Processing important information closer to where it is generated, rather than depending entirely on a remote server.

Worker Safety

Exploring wearable and portable technology capable of communicating worker and environmental data.

Mining Network Mapping

Software visualization of underground network nodes, communication paths, and safety events.

Research→ Prototype→ Laboratory Testing→ Controlled Field Testing→ Industry Validation→ Deployment

LinkMine is currently an R&D initiative. The system is being developed and tested as a research prototype and is not presented as a certified mine-safety system.
